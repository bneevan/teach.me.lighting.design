# Stage Lighting Design for Live Dance Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _A Method of Lighting the Stage_ by Stanley McCandless (Theatre Arts Books)](https://archive.org/details/methodoflighting00mcca)
  The foundational blueprint for all modern theatrical lighting design. Introduces the 45-degree angle system and the four controllable properties of light. Use for: foundational angle geometry, acting area divisions, and warm/cool contrast principles.
- [Book: _Stage Lighting: The Fundamentals_ by Richard Dunham (Routledge)](https://www.routledge.com/Stage-Lighting-The-Fundamentals/Dunham/p/book/9780367468163)
  Modern comprehensive textbook covering instrument optics, photometrics, electrical distribution, and color theory. Use for: instrument selection (Fresnels vs. ERS), beam calculations, and stage paperwork.
- [Book: _Designing with Light: An Introduction to Stage Lighting_ by J. Michael Gillette & Michael McNamara (McGraw-Hill)](https://www.mheducation.com)
  Clear, accessible breakdown of the design process, script/choreography analysis, lighting plots, and cue sheets. Use for: practical drafting and cue sheet construction.
- [Article: "Lighting for Dance" by Linda Essig (Lighting and the Design Idea)](https://www.routledge.com)
  Authoritative guidance on why dance lighting differs from traditional theater (prioritizing side-light sculpting of the torso and limbs over flat front visibility). Use for: boom placement and shin-buster angles.
- [Manual: _USITT Recommended Practice for Theatrical Lighting Design Graphics_ (USITT)](https://www.usitt.org)
  The industry-standard drafting symbols and documentation format. Use for: standard symbols on lighting plots and instrument schedules.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [ControlBooth Forum](https://www.controlbooth.com)
  The premier online community for theatre technicians and lighting designers, heavily moderated with seasoned pros and educators. Use for: practical troubleshooting, gear recommendations, and rigging safety checks.
- [r/lightingdesign (Reddit)](https://reddit.com/r/lightingdesign)
  Active community of concert, corporate, and theatrical lighting programmers and designers. Use for: console programming questions, DMX troubleshooting, and fixture comparisons.
- [r/techtheatre (Reddit)](https://reddit.com/r/techtheatre)
  Broad community of stage technicians, production managers, and designers. Use for: budget venue setups, makeshift rigging solutions, and cue-calling etiquette.
- Local Indian Cultural Venues & Sabhas (e.g., Narada Gana Sabha, Kalakshetra, Chowdiah Memorial, NCPA Mumbai)
  Crucial for real-world wisdom: observing how veteran Indian lighting masters illuminate Bharatanatyam, Kathak, and Odissi dancers without washing out silk sarees or blinding the front rows.
