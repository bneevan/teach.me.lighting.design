Stage Lighting Design Studio

Curriculum, interactive lessons, reference sheets, and design records for live dance performance lighting in India.

Workspace Mission

Design clean, three-dimensional, and emotionally resonant lighting for live dance performances (Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi, and contemporary dance) in Indian auditoriums and sabhas (100–200 seats)—from first principles to rigging, plotting, and live cueing.
