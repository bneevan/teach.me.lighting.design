User Notes & Working Preferences

Pedagogical direction, terminology standards, and venue-specific technical considerations.

Working Context & Decisions

  • Topic: Stage Lighting Design for Live Shows.
  • Terminology: Strictly use industry-standard "Lighting Design" and "Lighting Designer" (avoid colloquial "light designing").
  • Specific Domain Focus: Live dance performances in India (Indian classical like Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi, semi-classical, and contemporary dance).
  • Venue Scale: Intimate to mid-sized auditoriums / sabhas / halls (~100–200 seats).
  • Scope Exclusion: Photography, studio strobes, and camera flash setups dropped completely.
  • Background / Starting Point: Starting from scratch with no prior technical lighting or rigging experience.
  • Pedagogical Track: Option A — Start with the universal physics and perceptual properties of light (intensity, color, distribution/angle, movement) and form rendering on the body, then build systematically into stage fixtures, plots, DMX control, and live cueing.

Key Cultural & Technical Factors

Sabha Venue & Costuming Nuances
  • Micro-expression Visibility: Need crystal-clear facial visibility (Abhinaya) and hand gestures (Mudras) without blinding, flat frontal washout.
  • Footwork Definition: Intricate footwork (Tala, Salangai/Ghungroos) requires deliberate low/side light angles (shin-busters).
  • Specular Glare Management: High-reflectance costumes (Kanjeevaram silks, gold zari) and polished temple jewelry require careful intensity and color temperature balance.
